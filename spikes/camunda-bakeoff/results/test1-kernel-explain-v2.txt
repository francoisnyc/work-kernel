$ pli type explain --draft purchase@2 kernel/types-v2/purchase.v2.yaml
purchase@2

Key
- the requester: the person an Item is for: whoever created it, or the person they created it for.
- a customer: a person outside the organisation.
- an agent: a program acting for a person; it may do only what both it and that person may do, and a field that names a person never names an agent.
- the kernel: the system that runs the rules of every Type; it is never a person.
- a hold: while a check or a reaction decides a move, the Item waits where it is; that wait is a hold.

Fields
- `f_amount`: a whole number
- `f_vendor`: text
- `f_cost_centre`: text
- `f_justification`: text
- `f_manager`: a person
- `f_claimed_by`: a person
- `f_summary`: text
- `f_policy`: one of ok, check or fail
- `f_flags`: text
- `f_reason`: text
- `f_order_id`: text

In short
1. A purchase starts in draft.
2. It moves only as listed below; any other move is refused.

Who migrates Items to it
3. While this is the latest version of purchase, anyone with the role purchase_admin may migrate an open purchase of an older version to it, once it fits.

Who writes what
4. Only the kernel writes `f_manager`: `manager_check`.
5. Only holders of finance write `f_claimed_by`.
6. Only holders of preparer write `f_summary`.
7. Only holders of preparer write `f_policy`.
8. Only holders of preparer write `f_flags`.
9. Only the kernel writes `f_order_id`: `place_order`.
10. No agent writes its fields, except one that names a role the agent holds.

draft (to do)
11. The requester moves it from draft to manager_review, once `f_amount`, `f_vendor`, `f_cost_centre` and `f_justification` are set, only if `f_amount` is more than 0.
12. Asking for this move opens a hold instead: `manager_check` calls `hr.manager_of@1` with onBehalfOf = who it was created for, createdBy = who created it; if it allows, or corrects `f_manager`, the move happens; it may deny it (unknown_employee, no_eligible_approver); with no answer within 15 minutes the move fails.
13. While that hold is open, nothing moves it.

manager_review (waiting)
14. While it is in manager_review, it waits on whoever `f_manager` names.
15. While it is in manager_review, nobody changes `f_amount`, `f_vendor`, `f_cost_centre`, `f_justification` or `f_manager`.
16. Whoever `f_manager` names, but never anyone who took part in creating it, moves it from manager_review to approved, only if `f_amount` is less than 1000.
17. Whoever `f_manager` names, but never anyone who took part in creating it, moves it from manager_review to finance_queue, only if `f_amount` is at least 1000.
18. Whoever `f_manager` names, but never anyone who took part in creating it, moves it from manager_review to rejected, once the mover gives `f_reason` in the move itself.

finance_queue (waiting)
19. While it is in finance_queue, it waits on anyone with the role finance.
20. While it is in finance_queue, nobody changes `f_amount`, `f_vendor`, `f_cost_centre`, `f_justification`, `f_manager`, `f_summary`, `f_policy` or `f_flags`.
21. Anyone with the role finance, but never anyone who took part in creating it, moves it from finance_queue to finance_review, once `f_claimed_by` is set.

finance_review (active)
22. Whoever moves it into finance_review claims it: `f_claimed_by` names them, and only they move it on from there, unless a move releases the claim.
23. While it is in finance_review, it waits on whoever claimed it.
24. While it is in finance_review, nobody changes `f_amount`, `f_vendor`, `f_cost_centre`, `f_justification`, `f_manager`, `f_summary`, `f_policy` or `f_flags`.
25. Anyone with the role finance moves it from finance_review to finance_queue, which releases the claim; the move clears `f_claimed_by`.
26. Anyone with the role finance, but never anyone who took part in creating it, moves it from finance_review to approved, only if `f_amount` is at most 5000.
27. Anyone with the role finance, but never anyone who took part in creating it, moves it from finance_review to cfo_review, only if `f_amount` is more than 5000.
28. Anyone with the role finance, but never anyone who took part in creating it, moves it from finance_review to rejected, once the mover gives `f_reason` in the move itself.

cfo_review (waiting)
29. While it is in cfo_review, it waits on anyone with the role cfo.
30. While it is in cfo_review, nobody changes `f_amount`, `f_vendor`, `f_cost_centre`, `f_justification`, `f_manager`, `f_claimed_by`, `f_summary`, `f_policy` or `f_flags`.
31. Anyone with the role cfo, but never anyone who took part in creating it, moves it from cfo_review to approved.
32. Anyone with the role cfo, but never anyone who took part in creating it, moves it from cfo_review to rejected, once the mover gives `f_reason` in the move itself.

approved (done)
33. On entering approved, `place_order` calls `erp.create_invoice@1` with customerId = `f_vendor`, amount = `f_amount` and writes what it made into `f_order_id`, once per entry, even if it has moved on.
34. While it is in approved, nobody changes `f_amount`, `f_vendor`, `f_cost_centre`, `f_justification`, `f_manager`, `f_claimed_by`, `f_summary`, `f_policy`, `f_flags` or `f_reason`.
35. Nothing leaves approved.

rejected (canceled)
36. On entering rejected, `notify_rejected` calls `email.notify@1` with to = the requester, once per entry, even if it has moved on.
37. While it is in rejected, nobody changes `f_amount`, `f_vendor`, `f_cost_centre`, `f_justification`, `f_manager`, `f_claimed_by`, `f_summary`, `f_policy`, `f_flags`, `f_reason` or `f_order_id`.
38. Nothing leaves rejected.

Who sees it
39. A customer who sees it sees none of its fields, only its header.
40. No one moves it to another container or narrows who sees it.

Actions it calls
- `hr.manager_of@1` (a check, low risk): Finds who approves for a requester: their manager in the HR directory. The requester is onBehalfOf when set, else createdBy.
- `erp.create_invoice@1` (an action with side effects, high risk): Creates an invoice in the ERP for a customer and an amount, once per idempotency key.
- `email.notify@1` (an action with side effects, medium risk): Sends one message to one person or agent; the hook's ID names the message, and the Item goes with it.

Not covered by this reading
- Who can see a given purchase: that is set for each Item and its container, not by its Type.
- Who may create a purchase, and where, and who may move or narrow one, once a later version exists: the latest version of purchase decides, for every version.
- How the kernel keeps its promises (retries, each write made once, recovery after a failure): the same for every Type.
