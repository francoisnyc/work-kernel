$ pli type explain --draft purchase@2 kernel/types-v2/purchase.v2.yaml
purchase@2

Key
- the requester: the person an Item is for: whoever created it, or the person they created it for.
- a customer: a person outside the organisation.
- an agent: a program acting for a person; it may do only what both it and that person may do, and a field that names a person never names an agent.
- the kernel: the system that runs the rules of every Type; it is never a person.
- a hold: while a check or a reaction decides a move, the Item waits where it is; that wait is a hold.

Fields
- `f_amount`: a whole number
- `f_vendor`: text
- `f_cost_centre`: text
- `f_justification`: text
- `f_manager`: a person
- `f_claimed_by`: a person
- `f_summary`: text
- `f_policy`: one of ok, check or fail
- `f_flags`: text
- `f_reason`: text
- `f_order_id`: text

In short
1. A purchase starts in draft.
2. It moves only as listed below; any other move is refused.

Who writes what
3. Only the kernel writes `f_manager`: `manager_check`.
4. Only holders of finance write `f_claimed_by`.
5. Only holders of preparer write `f_summary`.
6. Only holders of preparer write `f_policy`.
7. Only holders of preparer write `f_flags`.
8. Only the kernel writes `f_order_id`: `place_order`.

draft (to do)
9. The requester moves it from draft to manager_review, once `f_amount`, `f_vendor`, `f_cost_centre` and `f_justification` are set, only if `f_amount` is more than 0.
10. Asking for this move opens a hold instead: `manager_check` calls `hr.manager_of@1` with onBehalfOf = who it was created for, createdBy = who created it; if it allows, or corrects `f_manager`, the move happens; it may deny it (unknown_employee, no_eligible_approver); with no answer within 15 minutes the move fails.
11. While that hold is open, nothing moves it.

manager_review (waiting)
12. While it is in manager_review, nobody changes `f_amount`, `f_vendor`, `f_cost_centre`, `f_justification` or `f_manager`.
13. Whoever `f_manager` names, but never anyone who took part in creating it, moves it from manager_review to approved, only if `f_amount` is less than 1000.
14. Whoever `f_manager` names, but never anyone who took part in creating it, moves it from manager_review to finance_queue, only if `f_amount` is at least 1000.
15. Whoever `f_manager` names, but never anyone who took part in creating it, moves it from manager_review to rejected, once `f_reason` is set.

finance_queue (waiting)
16. While it is in finance_queue, nobody changes `f_amount`, `f_vendor`, `f_cost_centre`, `f_justification` or `f_manager`.
17. Anyone with the role finance, but never anyone who took part in creating it, moves it from finance_queue to finance_review, once `f_claimed_by` is set.

finance_review (active)
18. While it is in finance_review, nobody changes `f_amount`, `f_vendor`, `f_cost_centre`, `f_justification` or `f_manager`.
19. Anyone with the role finance moves it from finance_review to finance_queue; the move clears `f_claimed_by`.
20. Anyone with the role finance, but never anyone who took part in creating it, moves it from finance_review to approved, only if `f_amount` is at most 5000.
21. Anyone with the role finance, but never anyone who took part in creating it, moves it from finance_review to cfo_review, only if `f_amount` is more than 5000.
22. Anyone with the role finance, but never anyone who took part in creating it, moves it from finance_review to rejected, once `f_reason` is set.

cfo_review (waiting)
23. While it is in cfo_review, nobody changes `f_amount`, `f_vendor`, `f_cost_centre`, `f_justification`, `f_manager` or `f_claimed_by`.
24. Anyone with the role cfo, but never anyone who took part in creating it, moves it from cfo_review to approved.
25. Anyone with the role cfo, but never anyone who took part in creating it, moves it from cfo_review to rejected, once `f_reason` is set.

approved (done)
26. On entering approved, `place_order` calls `erp.create_invoice@1` with customerId = `f_vendor`, amount = `f_amount` and writes what it made into `f_order_id`, once per entry, even if it has moved on.
27. While it is in approved, nobody changes `f_amount`, `f_vendor`, `f_cost_centre`, `f_justification`, `f_manager`, `f_claimed_by`, `f_summary`, `f_policy`, `f_flags` or `f_reason`.
28. Nothing leaves approved.

rejected (canceled)
29. On entering rejected, `notify_rejected` calls `email.notify@1` with to = the requester, once per entry, even if it has moved on.
30. While it is in rejected, nobody changes `f_amount`, `f_vendor`, `f_cost_centre`, `f_justification`, `f_manager`, `f_claimed_by`, `f_summary`, `f_policy`, `f_flags`, `f_reason` or `f_order_id`.
31. Nothing leaves rejected.

Who sees it
32. A customer who sees it sees none of its fields, only its header.
33. No one moves it to another container or narrows who sees it.

Actions it calls
- `hr.manager_of@1` (a check, low risk): Finds who approves for a requester: their manager in the HR directory. The requester is onBehalfOf when set, else createdBy.
- `erp.create_invoice@1` (an action with side effects, high risk): Creates an invoice in the ERP for a customer and an amount, once per idempotency key.
- `email.notify@1` (an action with side effects, medium risk): Sends one message to one person or agent; the hook's ID names the message, and the Item goes with it.

Not covered by this reading
- Who can see a given purchase: that is set for each Item and its container, not by its Type.
- How the kernel keeps its promises (retries, each write made once, recovery after a failure): the same for every Type.
