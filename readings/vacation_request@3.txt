vacation_request@3

Key
- the requester: the person an Item is for: whoever created it, or the person they created it for.
- a customer: a person outside the organisation.
- an agent: a program acting for a person; it may do only what both it and that person may do, and a field that names a person never names an agent.
- the kernel: the system that runs the rules of every Type; it is never a person.
- the timer: the kernel's clock; it makes only the moves a Type gives it.
- a hold: while a check or a reaction decides a move, the Item waits where it is; that wait is a hold.

Fields
- `f_start`: a date
- `f_end`: a date
- `f_days`: a whole number
- `f_leave_type`: one of annual, unpaid or other
- `f_approver`: a person
- `f_comment`: text

In short
1. A vacation_request starts in draft.
2. It moves only as listed below; any other move is refused.

Who writes what
3. Only the kernel writes `f_approver`: `hr_check`.

Links
4. Only the kernel links it to a leave_balance as `balance`, at most one; a leave_balance sees it as `requests`.

draft (to do)
5. The requester moves it from draft to submitted, once `f_start`, `f_end`, `f_days` and `f_leave_type` are set, only if `f_end` is on or after `f_start`, `f_start` is on or after today and `f_days` is more than 0.
6. Asking for this move opens a hold instead: `hr_check` calls `hr.manager_of@1` with onBehalfOf = who it was created for, createdBy = who created it; if it allows, or corrects `f_approver`, the move happens; it may deny it (unknown_employee, no_eligible_approver); with no answer within 15 minutes the move fails.
7. While that hold is open, the only move out of draft is to withdrawn.
8. The requester moves it from draft to withdrawn, even while a hold is open on it, which cancels the hold.

submitted (waiting)
9. On entering submitted, the kernel links `balance` to the requester's open leave_balance (the one whose `f_owner` is the requester); if there is none, or more than one, the move into submitted is refused.
10. While it is in submitted, nobody changes `f_start`, `f_end`, `f_days`, `f_leave_type` or `f_approver`.
11. 120 hours after it enters submitted, if it is still there, the timer moves it to expired; if a hold is open then, the timer waits for it to close and looks again.
12. 48 hours after it enters submitted, then every 24 hours, `remind_approver` calls `email.notify@1` with to = `f_approver`, at most 2 times while it stays there.
13. Whoever `f_approver` names, but never anyone who took part in creating it, moves it from submitted to approved.
14. Asking for this move opens a hold instead: `deduct` adds `f_days` to `f_used` on the leave_balance linked as `balance`; the move happens only if that Item accepts the write, else it is denied with that Item's reason.
15. While that hold is open, nobody changes `f_days`, and the only move out of submitted is to withdrawn.
16. Whoever `f_approver` names, but never anyone who took part in creating it, moves it from submitted to rejected.
17. The requester moves it from submitted to draft.
18. The requester moves it from submitted to withdrawn, even while a hold is open on it, which cancels the hold.
19. Only the timer moves it from submitted to expired.

approved (done)
20. On entering approved, `notify_approved` calls `email.notify@1` with to = the requester, once per entry, even if it has moved on.
21. While it is in approved, nobody changes `f_start`, `f_end`, `f_days`, `f_leave_type` or `f_approver`.
22. The requester moves it from approved to withdrawn, only if `f_start` is on or after today.

rejected (canceled)
23. On entering rejected, `notify_rejected` calls `email.notify@1` with to = the requester, once per entry, even if it has moved on.
24. While it is in rejected, nobody changes `f_start`, `f_end`, `f_days`, `f_leave_type` or `f_approver`.
25. Nothing leaves rejected.

withdrawn (canceled)
26. On entering withdrawn from approved, `refund` gives back what `deduct` added.
27. When `refund` has applied, `refund_email` calls `email.notify@1` with to = the requester.
28. Nothing leaves withdrawn.

expired (canceled)
29. On entering expired, `notify_expired` calls `email.notify@1` with to = the requester, once per entry, even if it has moved on.
30. Nothing leaves expired.

Who sees it
31. A customer who sees it sees none of its fields, only its header.
32. Anyone who can see it moves it to another container or narrows who sees it.

Actions it calls
- `hr.manager_of@1` (a check, low risk): Finds who approves for a requester: their manager in the HR directory. The requester is onBehalfOf when set, else createdBy.
- `email.notify@1` (an action with side effects, medium risk): Sends one message to one person or agent; the hook's ID names the message, and the Item goes with it.

Not covered by this reading
- Who can see a given vacation_request: that is set for each Item and its container, not by its Type.
- How the kernel keeps its promises (retries, each write made once, recovery after a failure): the same for every Type.
